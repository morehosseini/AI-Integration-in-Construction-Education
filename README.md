# AI Integration in AEC Education
### Frameworks, Tools, and Pedagogical Applications

Companion repository and website for the book **AI Integration in AEC Education: Frameworks, Tools, and Pedagogical Applications** (Routledge / Taylor & Francis), edited by M. Reza Hosseini, Mohamad Kassem, Hongtao Dang, Miroslaw J. Skibniewski and Heng Li. This repository hosts companion material (appendices, code, notebooks and figure files) for the 15 chapters.

**Book status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production.

**Website:** <https://morehosseini.github.io/AI-Integration-in-Construction-Education/>

---

## 📚 Book Structure

### Part I: Foundations
- **Chapter 1:** Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Simple Terms
- **Chapter 2:** Integrating AI Literacy into Construction Education: Definitions, Frameworks and Lessons Learned
- **Chapter 3:** The Impact of Generative Artificial Intelligence on Built Environment and Engineering Education
- **Chapter 4:** When Technical Outputs No Longer Demonstrate Competence: A Capability Framework for AI-Enabled Built Environment Education
- **Chapter 5:** Beyond the Algorithm: Understanding Your Role in Responsible AI Deployment in Construction

### Part II: Teaching, Curriculum and Professional Formation
- **Chapter 6:** AI in Construction: Identifying Competencies and Analysing Teaching Methods
- **Chapter 7:** Embedding AI in Existing and New Curricula: A Survey-Based Study for AEC Education in Germany
- **Chapter 8:** Guidelines and Benefits of GPT/LLM in Construction Management Education
- **Chapter 9:** Large Language Models (LLMs) for Adaptive and Personalised Learning in Construction Education

### Part III: Data, Infrastructure and Applied AI Capabilities
- **Chapter 10:** The Digital Blueprint: Structuring Heterogeneous Construction Data for Artificial Intelligence
- **Chapter 11:** Data Centres for the AI Era: A Construction Manager’s Guide
- **Chapter 12:** AI-Assisted Programming in Construction Education
- **Chapter 13:** Digital Twins as Pedagogical Tools for Construction 4.0
- **Chapter 14:** Agentic AI in Construction: Frameworks, Use Cases and Practical Governance

### Part IV: Capstone
- **Chapter 15:** Operationalising AI Ethics in Construction: Principles, Governance and Tools
