# Chapter 10 — The Digital Blueprint: Structuring Heterogeneous Construction Data for Artificial Intelligence

**Authors:** M. Reza Hosseini, Ruoxin Xiong, Amir Soroush, Tim Mumford  
**Part:** Part III: Data, Infrastructure and Applied AI Capabilities  
**Status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production

## About this chapter

Artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping the architecture, engineering and construction (AEC) sector, yet its success depends far less on algorithms than on the data consumed by those algorithms. This chapter argues that construction AI readiness is fundamentally a data problem: the sector’s information is heterogeneous, fragmented across formats and project phases, unevenly governed and highly variable in quality. Beginning from the observation that construction is data abundant yet insight constrained, the chapter characterises construction data through the 5Vs framework and a working taxonomy of data types and modalities, maps data formats across the seven-phase project life cycle and examines the systemic challenges impeding AI, such as mixed units, inconsistent schemas, unstructured text and coordinate system mismatches. It then connects these challenges to the international standards and quality dimensions that make data trustworthy, interoperable and governable. The central message for educators and students is that preparing construction data through their structure, governance and quality assurance is the indispensable precondition for AI, not a secondary concern.
