# Chapter 2 — Integrating AI Literacy into Construction Education: Definitions, Frameworks and Lessons Learned

**Authors:** Jiun-Yao Cheng, Masoud Gheisari, Idris Jeelani  
**Part:** Part I: Foundations  
**Status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production

## About this chapter

Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming industries globally, including the construction industry. In response, educators and researchers increasingly emphasise the importance of developing AI literacy across academic disciplines and professional levels. Due to its ability to influence how individuals critically, ethically and effectively engage with AI technologies and make informed decisions with AI support, AI literacy is becoming increasingly important. Recognising that different domains require competencies tailored to each domain, scholars have recently begun advocating for domain-specific AI literacy, although this conversation is still limited in construction education.

This chapter initiates a discussion on construction domain-specific AI literacy, exploring how it can serve as a foundation for integrating AI into construction curricula. It presents one of the first systematic approaches to defining an AI literacy framework tailored for the construction domain, emphasising its role in cultivating a workforce prepared to navigate an AI-integrated industry. To demonstrate practical implementation, the chapter also introduces a use case, entitled AI4Safety, to showcase how a tailored AI literacy framework can inform curriculum design in construction safety education.
