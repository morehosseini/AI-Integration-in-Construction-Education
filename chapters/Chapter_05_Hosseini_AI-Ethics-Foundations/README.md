# Chapter 5 — Beyond the Algorithm: Understanding Your Role in Responsible AI Deployment in Construction

**Authors:** M. Reza Hosseini, Abdolmajid Erfani, Fahimeh Abedi, Deniz Besiktepe, Heng Li, Miroslaw J. Skibniewski  
**Part:** Part I: Foundations  
**Status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production

## About this chapter

This chapter establishes foundational knowledge for responsibly implementing artificial intelligence (AI) technologies in construction projects. It frames responsible AI not as an external constraint but as a practical extension of construction management’s established duties of care, competence, public protection, transparency and accountability. The chapter develops this perspective through five components. Firstly, it clarifies the related but non-identical concepts of human-centred, ethical, trustworthy and responsible AI, while identifying the relationships adopted for this chapter. Secondly, it establishes AI ecosystem roles and explains why construction organisations normally act as deployers, while recognising that role classification depends on what an organisation actually does. Thirdly, it examines historical and professional foundations of construction ethics. Fourthly, it compares major international AI governance instruments, distinguishing binding law from voluntary principles and technical standards. Finally, it bridges traditional construction ethics to AI-enabled practice through construction-specific scenarios and controls. The companion Chapter 15 builds on this foundation with an integrated principles–governance–tools implementation framework.
