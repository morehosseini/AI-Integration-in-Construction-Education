# Chapter 7 — Embedding AI in Existing and New Curricula: A Survey-Based Study for AEC Education in Germany

**Authors:** Niels Bartels, Tobias Maile, Reinhard Wimmer  
**Part:** Part II: Teaching, Curriculum and Professional Formation  
**Status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production

## About this chapter

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly shaping architecture, engineering and construction (AEC) education by enabling new forms of learning, automation and data-driven decision making. This chapter presents the results from a survey of 409 participants (students, professors and non-professorial academic staff, such as research assistants) from 23 German universities, examining their perceptions of AI’s role in teaching, learning and academic work. The findings show broad support for integrating AI into foundational curricula, particularly among professors (over 60%) and academic staff (over 50%). While participants report potential benefits for personalised learning and thesis quality, ratings vary regarding ethical risks, data privacy and the impact of AI on professional responsibilities. Faculty members express stronger concerns about data governance and academic integrity, whereas students tend to be more optimistic about AI’s benefits. The chapter concludes with strategic recommendations for curriculum development, pedagogy and teacher training. The limitations relate to sample composition and reliance on self-reported data, highlighting the need for further empirical research to support evidence-based AI adoption in higher education.
