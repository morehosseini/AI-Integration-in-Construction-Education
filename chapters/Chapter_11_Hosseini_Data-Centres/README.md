# Chapter 11 — Data Centres for the AI Era: A Construction Manager’s Guide

**Authors:** M. Reza Hosseini, Ania Khodabakhshian, Brooks H. Smith, Abdolmajid Erfani, Manideep Tummalapudi  
**Part:** Part III: Data, Infrastructure and Applied AI Capabilities  
**Status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production

## About this chapter

The explosive growth of artificial intelligence (AI) has transformed data centres from specialised facilities into critical infrastructure forming the physical foundation of the global digital economy. Construction engineers and project managers are primary actors in creating data centres, yet resources that inform construction engineers and built environment professionals about these assets remain limited. This chapter addresses this gap by presenting a construction-management-oriented synthesis of global best practices, drawing on examples from Australia, the United States (US) and Europe. Unlike traditional commercial buildings, AI-era data centres operate as ‘intelligence factories’ with extraordinary demands: floor loads substantially exceeding conventional commercial requirements, power consumption at scales rivalling small cities and cooling systems of industrial complexity. The chapter covers development trends and market drivers; facility typologies and classification systems; structural and civil engineering requirements; mechanical cooling and electrical power system coordination; environmental impacts and sustainability solutions; contemporary construction delivery methods; and the cost benchmarking, risk management, quality control and commissioning processes specific to mission-critical facilities. Technical concepts from adjacent disciplines are presented at a level appropriate for coordination and project management rather than detailed design.
