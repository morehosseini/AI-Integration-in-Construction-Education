# Chapter 8 — Guidelines and Benefits of GPT/LLM in Construction Management Education

**Authors:** Luciana Debs, Deniz Besiktepe  
**Part:** Part II: Teaching, Curriculum and Professional Formation  
**Status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production

## About this chapter

This chapter examines the emerging role of Generative Pre-trained Transformers (GPT) and Large Language Models (LLMs) in construction education. It reviews how AI-powered chatbots can support curriculum development, assessment design, personalised feedback, student tutoring, collaborative learning and instructional efficiency. The chapter also discusses practical applications in construction courses, including the development of assignments, rubrics, course-specific chatbots and scenario-based learning activities. Alongside these benefits, it addresses key challenges related to accuracy, hallucinations, academic integrity, data privacy, algorithmic bias, accessibility and over-reliance on automated tools. Drawing on prior research and classroom examples, the chapter proposes practical guidelines for the responsible and pedagogically sound use of GPT and LLM technologies. It emphasises the importance of instructor oversight, prompt literacy, transparency and alignment with learning objectives to ensure that these tools strengthen, rather than replace, critical thinking, professional judgement and authentic learning in construction education.
