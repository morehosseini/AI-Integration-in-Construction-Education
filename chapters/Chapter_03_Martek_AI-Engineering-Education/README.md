# Chapter 3 — The Impact of Generative Artificial Intelligence on Built Environment and Engineering Education

**Authors:** Igor Martek, Mehdi Amirkhani, Chayakan Siamphukdee  
**Part:** Part I: Foundations  
**Status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production

## About this chapter

Generative artificial intelligence (AI) is emerging as the most disruptive technological breakthrough of the current generation. This is particularly true in education where its repercussions will forever change the ways in which architecture, construction management and civil engineering are taught. Innovations exist to improve society, yet while AI’s imprint is expected to be far-sweeping and positive, many hold grave apprehensions about its impact. Numerous studies exploring this dichotomy have already been published. This chapter digests the debate and presents the pros and cons of AI’s impact on education from the perspectives of the student, the educator and the administrator. It is perhaps surprising that much of the research finds significant faults with AI while, at the same time, conceding its inevitable rise in, and overpowering of, the educational landscape.
