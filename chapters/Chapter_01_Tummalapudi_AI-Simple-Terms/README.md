# Chapter 1 — Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Simple Terms

**Authors:** Manideep Tummalapudi, M. Reza Hosseini, Wei Wu, Brad Hyatt  
**Part:** Part I: Foundations  
**Status:** Manuscript delivered to the publisher, November 2026; in production

## About this chapter

This chapter introduces the fundamental concepts of artificial intelligence (AI), with a focus on AI’s relevance to the architecture, engineering and construction (AEC) industry. It provides a foundational understanding of AI by defining key terms, explaining the critical role of data, distinguishing between narrow AI and general AI, and introducing generative AI and agentic AI as emerging paradigms. The chapter examines the primary branches of AI: Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing and Computer Vision, each of which is illustrated with practical examples from construction practice. Common AI-related ethical considerations are identified, such as data bias, privacy concerns and the need for transparent and accountable AI systems. Emphasis is placed on AI literacy for the construction workforce, positioning technological proficiency as a critical competency for the industry’s future workforce. Through this introductory exploration, the chapter establishes the conceptual framework for understanding the integration of AI into construction education and professional practice.
